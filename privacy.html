Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

Daily Notes Sync is a private, single-owner utility. It is not a public service and is not intended for distribution or use by others.

Google user data

Access to Google Calendar, Gmail, Drive, Sheets and Docs is granted only by the account owner and is used solely to perform actions requested by that owner (for example, listing calendar events or sending an email on their behalf). Google user data is never sold, never shared with third parties, and never used for advertising or marketing purposes.

Storage

Access tokens and processed data are stored exclusively on the owner's own private infrastructure and are not transferred anywhere else.

Revocation

Access can be revoked at any time in the connected Google account under Security → "Third-party apps with account access".
